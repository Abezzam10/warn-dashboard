04 / METHODS & LIMITS

What these signals can tell you.

Public notices, not the whole labor market

WARN notices are announcements under state and federal reporting rules. Many job losses are not covered. Worker totals are announced positions, may include possible duplicates and omit unknown headcounts. They are not unique people or realized losses.

Source rows have a feed ID, not a verified agency-document URL. Free-text location is not a reliable county field; industry, visa status and causal explanations are unavailable.

Source: WARN Feed by APVentureEngine, CC BY 4.0. This dashboard normalizes dates and headcounts and flags possible duplicates. It does not independently verify every agency notice.

No hidden server

Python scripts and the daily scheduled workflow live in the same GitHub repository. Standard GitHub Actions runners refresh the static JSON. Pages serves it; filters, saved views and optional AI run in your browser.

No account or analytics are added by this app. Views are stored locally. Shared links expose their filters. GitHub and Hugging Face see download requests; news links leave the site. Free services have quotas and may change terms.

GDELT links are approximate employer matches, not evidence of a layoff's cause. Economics is context only. FRED is optional and unavailable without a key; no credentials are in this site.
