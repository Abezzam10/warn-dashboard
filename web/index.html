05 / GOOD TO KNOW

What these numbers mean

  • Two different counts. The archive tracks WARN notices - announcements companies file before mass layoffs. The forecast tracks the government's monthly JOLTS count of layoffs and firings. They measure different things, so their numbers are never directly comparable.
  • Nothing here is about you or your company. The forecast is one national total, not a prediction for any person or employer.
  • Limits stay visible. WARN notices miss many real layoffs, record no visa status and no reasons; the forecast is an experiment whose tests flatter it. Doubt is stated where it exists.
  • Open and free. Data: WARN Feed (CC BY 4.0), California EDD, US Bureau of Labor Statistics. All code and data in a public repository, refreshed daily by a free automated job. No accounts, no tracking.
