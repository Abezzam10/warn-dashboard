05 / HOW IT WORKS

What these numbers mean - and what they don't

Two different layoff counts

WARN notices (the archive) are announcements companies file before mass layoffs. Totals are announced positions: some filings lack a headcount and some may be duplicates. Many layoffs never require a notice, so the archive misses real job losses. WARN records no visa status, no reliable industry, and no reason for the layoff.

JOLTS layoffs and discharges (the forecast) is a national monthly count from the Bureau of Labor Statistics. It also includes firings and other discharges, so it is broader than "layoffs" in the news. The two measures count different things - never compare their numbers directly.

Archive source: WARN Feed by APVentureEngine (CC BY 4.0), which republishes state agency filings. California check: California EDD official reports.

How the site runs

All code and data live in a public GitHub repository. A free scheduled job refreshes the data daily; your own browser does the filtering and counting. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking.

News matching is switched off: the free news API rate-limited this project, so the page shows no news links rather than unreliable ones.
